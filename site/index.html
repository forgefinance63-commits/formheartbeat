How it works

Three steps. No plugin to install.

FormHeartbeat works from the outside, like a visitor would. Nothing runs on your clients' servers.

1

Add the pages

Send us the contact or quote page URLs for the client sites you manage.

2

We check them on a schedule

Each check loads the page and looks for the expected form markup from the supported plugin.

3

You get an email when it breaks

An alert after repeated failures, not one-off blips — and a recovery email when it's back.

What FormHeartbeat does — and doesn't — do

We'd rather you know exactly what you're getting.

It does

  • Check that each contact page loads and the form is present
  • Email you after repeated failures, plus a recovery notice
  • Avoid duplicate alerts for the same ongoing problem
  • Flag pages it can't check as UNVERIFIABLE

It doesn't

  • Submit your forms — no test leads, no spam in your clients' inboxes
  • Verify that submissions reach email, a CRM or a spreadsheet
  • Guarantee a fixed check interval or alert time during the pilot
  • Replace uptime monitoring or a full QA process

FAQ

Questions

Do I need to install anything on client sites?

No. Checks run from outside, the same way a visitor loads the page.

Will it send test submissions?

No. FormHeartbeat never submits forms, so it won't create fake leads or trigger client notifications.

How often are pages checked?

Pages are checked on a recurring schedule. During the pilot we don't promise a fixed interval or alert time; we'll share the actual check history with you.

What does UNVERIFIABLE mean?

The page blocked our check (for example with bot protection), so we can't tell whether the form is there. We report that instead of a false OK or FAIL.

What happens after 14 days?

We ask whether you want to continue at $19/month. If you don't reply or say no, monitoring stops and nothing is charged.

Is this an established product?

No — it's new, and we're running a small number of pilots with agencies. Your feedback shapes what gets built next.
