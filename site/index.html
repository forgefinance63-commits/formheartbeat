Know when a client's contact form quietly breaks.

A plugin update, theme change or caching tweak can remove a WordPress form or break its script without anyone noticing, until the client asks why leads stopped. FormHeartbeat checks your clients' contact pages from the outside and emails you when something changes.

What it checks

Supported form plugins

Contact Form 7, WPForms, Gravity Forms and Elementor forms are detected on the page you specify.

External, non-submitting checks

We load the page like a visitor and confirm the form and its required markup are present. We never fill in or submit anything.

Email alerts

An alert after repeated failures (not a single blip), one alert per incident, and a recovery notice when the form is back.

Three honest states

OK — the expected form was found on the page.
FAIL — the page loaded but the form is missing or broken, or the page is persistently unreachable.
UNVERIFIABLE — we could not reliably tell (for example bot protection or heavy script bundling). We report this instead of guessing.

Limits, stated up front

  • FormHeartbeat does not submit forms.
  • It does not verify that a submission reaches your inbox, CRM or any other destination. A form that is present can still fail on submit.
  • Check frequency is best-effort; we do not guarantee a fixed cadence or alert time.
  • It is an early-stage service run by a small team. Pilots help us decide what to build next.
